📊 MATCHING ALGORITHM (7 FACTORS)
═══════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════════

Level Compatibility (Most Important)
  • Same level:        +50 points
  • ±1 level away:     +25 points
  • 2+ levels away:    0 points

Shared Interests
  • Per matching interest: +15 points

User Reputation
  • reputation / 10 points
  • Minimum 10 points (100 reputation baseline)

Activity Level
  • totalSessions / 5 points
  • totalMinutes / 60 points

Quality of Past Conversations
  • Recent high-quality sessions increase score
  • Temporal decay over 30 days
  • Minimum 50% quality boost maintained

Repeat Match Preferences
  • Previous high rating (≥4.0): +40 points
  • Encourages relationship building

Safety Scoring
  • Warning flag: -30 points (visible, but available)
  • Suspended: -80 points (not available)
  • Banned: Removed from candidates entirely
